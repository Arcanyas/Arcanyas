## Some Public Repositories and Projects I have worked on 👀
- [50 plus and fabulous](https://github.com/Arcanyas/50-fab) - A website I worked on at the request of a personal trainer, I particularly enjoyed making the testimonials section! All assets images etc. have been kept locally as to maintain security. 
- [Land of the CPU](https://github.com/Arcanyas/CPUGame) - A game I made entirely using C++ and SDL2 (no game engine) --> all assets similar to 50 plus and fabulous have been kept locally and not uploaded to the repository.
- [Adaptive Insulin Dosing](https://github.com/Arcanyas/AdaptiveInsulinDosing) - An EnMPC combining LSTM and Random Forest forecasts with the Bergman physiological model, which predicts ideal insulin based on predicted glucose levels.
- [TSA for sit to stand](https://github.com/Arcanyas/TSASTSProject.git) - Mujoco simulation to simulate sit to stand and estimate torques and times for activation motors on a sit to stand exoskeleton to ensure the best efficiency.
